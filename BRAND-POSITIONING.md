# RoleVow — Product scope

RoleVow is a simple AI-assisted tabletop RPG tool. It supports the choices of Players and Game Masters; it is not a foundation model or new ruleset.

**Play your role, now it's your roll. Our vow? To make you say wow.**

The Negative Price Ultra-Guarantee is **NOT active**. The project does not claim a verified worldwide first. No measured speed, support or satisfaction claim is made before independent testing. A simulated €1 net payout and draft test limits of €20 per month and €100 lifetime are sandbox inputs only, not an offer. The term "free" describes RoleVow's own source access, not ChatGPT plans or limits.
