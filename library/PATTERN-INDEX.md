## Retrieval contract and classification

This is a map of **optional techniques**, not instructions to load or apply everything. Start with CORE and the active adapter. A **CORE candidate** is a near-universal invariant; a **PROTOCOL** has a specific trigger and procedure; a **MODIFIER** changes a protocol only in an authorized scope; a **LIBRARY-ONLY** technique is entirely optional. For a GM's practical problem first consult `MASTER-CRAFT-TOOLBOX.md`, then this index if a broader pattern is needed. Prefer one to three relevant entries. Mark source rules, table agreements and original suggestions separately; do not invent a citation or turn an evocative pattern into a new game rule.

## A. Prep, session design and choice

- **PREP VALUE DENSITY:** prioritize likely, consequential, reusable preparation; system competence creates a nonzero prep floor when needed.
- **SUNK-COST FIREWALL:** prepped content is not immune to legitimate player success.
- **ACTIVE PREMISE LEGIBILITY:** show an opportunity for action before asking for a decision.
- **FUNCTION-PRESERVING SCENARIO TRANSPOSITION:** move a scenario structure to another context only while keeping its actual decisions and consequences coherent.
- **DISPOSABLE SCENE SKELETON:** create situations players may abandon without destroying the adventure.
- **STAGED PREP COMPILER:** prep likely core deeply, plausible branches lightly and remote ones as risks only.
- **FOUR-LENS PREFLIGHT QA:** check player value, causal world, table usability and factual consistency.
- **DRAMATIC QUESTION EXIT GATE:** end the story unit when its key question is genuinely resolved, bypassed or transformed.
- **MEANINGFUL CHOICE CONTRACT:** know what changes if the player chooses another approach.
- **OPTION COMPRESSION:** simplify near-identical choices; avoid compulsory repetitive A/B/C menus.
- **STANDING DECISIONS:** preserve agreed tactical or procedural standing instructions until changed.
- **PLAYER-OWNED GOAL PORTFOLIO:** track what PCs actually want, not just the GM's assigned main quest.

## B. Revelation, clues and information

- **REVELATION COVERAGE LEDGER:** essential facts should have more than one plausible route to discovery where needed.
- **FAILURE-SENSITIVE HINT LADDER:** offer a proportionate next hint without rewriting what a previous failure established.
- **ATOMIC TRUTH UNIT:** record what is true independently of the paragraph that revealed it.
- **ACTIONABLE LORE REWARD:** a discovery should change understanding or create a real option.
- **KNOWLEDGE PROVENANCE:** separate GM truth, PC knowledge, NPC beliefs, rumors, inference and unknown.
- **CAUSAL TWIST / REVEAL INTEGRITY:** a reveal must not rely on secret facts chosen after their evidence was already used.

## C. NPCs, factions and social play

- **NPC STATE LADDER:** named recurring actors need more state than anonymous background figures, in proportion to relevance.
- **CONTEXT-INDEXED NPC RETRIEVAL:** recall an NPC's relevant status and goals when they reappear, not an invented full biography.
- **ORTHOGONAL NPC ATTRIBUTES:** vary motives, knowledge, voice and cues independently rather than recycling a single stereotype.
- **NPC VOICE / REGISTER LAYER:** preserve distinctive vocabulary, speech pattern and accessible language where established.
- **NPC VISUAL / BEHAVIOR ANCHORS:** use recurring features sparingly to help recognition.
- **LIVING FACTION FRONT:** actors advance plausible plans off-screen while preserving PC ability to intervene.
- **HIGH-STAKES NEGOTIATION EXCHANGE:** identify each side's goals, leverage, possible concessions and actual costs.
- **CONTRACT / DEAL STATE:** track offers, terms, parties, obligations and what has changed.
- **MORALE / RETREAT / SURRENDER:** opponents need not fight to the death and should behave according to what they know.

## D. Combat, hazards and contests

- **COMBAT INTEREST BUDGET:** make a few terrain features, decisions and objectives matter; minimize inert detail.
- **EFFECTIVE ACTION ECONOMY:** balance tension with actual system-defined turns, resources and number of actors.
- **PRECOMMITTED BOSS PHASE:** define important enemy stages before results could motivate retroactive changes.
- **SMART-ADVERSARY LEARNING LOOP:** enemies may learn from information they plausibly received, not from GM omniscience.
- **HAZARD INTENT TAXONOMY:** distinguish an environmental warning, a trap, an escalating clock and an unavoidable premise.
- **COMPLEX HAZARD STATE MACHINE:** define trigger, warning, progress, mitigation, effect and current state.
- **STEALTH AWARENESS STATE:** track what guards actually notice and when their behavior changes.
- **PARALLEL CONTEST PROGRESS:** where the system supports it, track opposed goals without manufacturing a fixed outcome.
- **ROLE-PARTITIONED SHARED CHALLENGE:** let different PCs contribute through distinct legitimate roles when appropriate.

## E. Skill use, character competence and approach

- **CHALLENGE LOCUS:** choose player skill, character skill or a hybrid explicitly for puzzles and social problems.
- **APPROACH-FIRST CHECK ROUTING:** identify intent and actual approach before naming a check or skill.
- **CHECK ECOLOGY / UNDERUSED CAPABILITY SURFACING:** use interesting capabilities when causally relevant, without a forced skill quota.
- **MULTI-APPROACH INFORMATION PAYLOAD:** looking, studying, recalling and asking can legitimately reveal different parts of a fact.

## F. Travel, exploration, place and environment

- **TRAVEL TURN:** destination, time/daylight, relevant weather, approach, navigation, discoveries, resources and resulting state.
- **OPERATIONAL SPATIAL SCALE:** use enough direction and distance detail to support the decision, not false precision.
- **EXPLORATION DISCOVERY LOOP:** movement, question, discovery, cost or changed capability.
- **PERSISTENT EXPOSURE LOOP:** weather, scarcity and danger accumulate only according to the active system and fiction.
- **EVIDENCE-DRIVEN MOBILE QUARRY:** a moving target's position follows clues, motives and realistic travel, not convenience.
- **MINIMUM VIABLE SETTLEMENT IDENTITY:** one or two defining features, relevant actors and available decisions.
- **LIGHTWEIGHT ROUTINE / AVAILABILITY:** NPC schedules, shops and entrances should change coherently with time.
- **SCENE CONTEXT ANCHOR:** maintain place, approximate time, current light and decisive environmental conditions.
- **PC-KNOWN GEOGRAPHY:** share local information a PC should know without arbitrary dice checks.
- **DAYLIGHT / PLANNING WINDOW:** let real lighting and travel choices create honest trade-offs.

## G. Institutions, domains and character arcs

- **PLAYER-OWNED INSTITUTION / DOMAIN:** when PCs manage a guild, ship, house or region, reflect actual decisions and costs at the agreed scale.
- **CHARACTER DISCOVERY:** reveal a character through consequential choices rather than forced biography.
- **CHARACTER-SAFE TENSION HOOK:** target fictional stakes without exploiting a real person's vulnerabilities.
- **INDIVIDUAL HIDDEN HOOK:** isolate what each PC/player is entitled to know.
- **PARTY SHARED HOOK:** make shared objectives credible rather than assuming cooperation.
- **ACTIONABLE REWARD:** rewards open capabilities, decisions or relationships; do not invent resources that were never awarded.

## H. Randomness, state and truth

- **PRECOMMITTED RANDOMNESS ENVELOPE:** fix meaningful bounds and sources before rolling, not after seeing the result.
- **RANDOM GENERATION GOVERNANCE:** use generated content only where its source and scope are authorized.
- **OPEN / CANDIDATE / CANON:** preserve draft possibilities separately from established facts.
- **PROVISIONAL RULING LEDGER:** track a temporary table decision and review it against the active source later.

## I. Atmosphere, identity and narrative presentation

- **ATMOSPHERE MICRO-BUNDLE:** use a few sensory cues directly relevant to place and choices.
- **SHOW THROUGH CONSEQUENCE:** establish the world's properties by how they affect actors and decisions.
- **SIGNATURE MECHANIC / IDENTITY ANCHOR:** recurring motifs should have coherent scope and not grant new mechanics by repetition.

## L. Campaign continuity and moving world

- **CAUSAL CONSEQUENCE PROPAGATION:** significant choices affect subsequent state without forcing a hidden master plot.
- **WORLD CLOCK:** actors advance according to established triggers and resources; no fake countdowns.
- **RIVAL PARTY:** rivals pursue their own plans and have only plausible information.
- **PUBLISHED ANTHOLOGY STITCHING:** link stand-alone adventures through earned consequences, relationships and world state.
- **CAMPAIGN CONTINUITY LOCUS:** decide whether continuity lives in PC, party, institution, front or world when cast changes.

## M. Themes, learning and meaning

- **THEME BEFORE SERMON:** moral or educational stakes should arise from real dilemmas, not lectures.
- **LEARNING THROUGH CONSEQUENCES:** demonstrate trade-offs through choices and outcomes without forcing agreement.
- **MULTI-PERSPECTIVE DILEMMA:** multiple defensible viewpoints improve agency over a single designated correct answer.
- **LIFE-SKILL THEME BANK:** consent, responsibility, trust, critical thinking, failure, deception and leadership can inspire conflict if fitting.
- **FUN-FIRST GUARDRAIL:** no personal development goal overrides players' agency, safety or enjoyment.

## N. Quality control and memory

- **PLAYER-FIRST PERSONALIZATION LOOP:** distinguish declared preferences from observed behavior and weak inference.
- **NARRATIVE / ORTHOGRAPHIC COHERENCE CHECK:** keep names, objects, quantity, timeline, location, grammar and semantics consistent.
- **SPOILER-SAFE VISIBLE PROCESS:** even intermediate output shown to players must not leak DM-only truth.
- **REPETITION / ESCALATION GOVERNOR:** repeated intentions should encounter a causally changed world, cost or opportunity—not a frozen loop.
- **SESSION ARC EXIT GATE:** close a meaningful unit instead of prolonging it indefinitely.
- **PERSISTENCE OFFER:** offer a versioned recap once per meaningful unit, role-safe and optional.
- **DECISION DENSITY CHECK:** compress sequences with little or no actual choice.
- **AI SETUP AS OPTIONAL LEVER:** more capable model/settings may help but are not a mandatory account requirement or a cure for errors.
- **FRICTION BUDGET:** extra questions, tools and media have costs; use only when expected value is greater.
- **PATTERN PROMOTION RULE:** promote a technique to CORE/PROTOCOL only after distinct evidence, not because it sounds useful.
- **LIBRARY USAGE RULE:** retrieve the minimum relevant subset on demand; leave unrelated patterns unloaded.

## O. Experience design and memorable outcomes

- **EXPERIENCE PROMISE:** know what type of game participants have actually accepted.
- **EARLY COMPETENCE BEAT:** allow an early meaningful use of the PC's genuine capabilities without granting automatic victory.
- **CALLBACK SEED LEDGER:** remember recurring established cues without making each one mandatory.
- **REVERSAL WITH FAIR SETUP:** surprising reversals must preserve causal clues and previous choices.
- **IRREVERSIBILITY MARK:** record a lasting change clearly rather than quietly undoing it.
- **EARNED PAYOFF:** a result matters because decisions and causes produced it.
- **PEAK MOMENT CANDIDATE:** offer an opportunity for an intense moment, not a scripted emotional demand.
- **END-STATE DELTA:** show how the world actually differs after the unit.
- **LOCAL HOPE / DURABLE VICTORY:** a small meaningful victory can persist without solving the entire campaign.
- **CAUSAL CONTINUATION HOOK:** continuation follows earned consequences, not forced FOMO.
- **MEMORABILITY GUARDRAIL:** do not add drama or jokes automatically because engagement is a metric.

## P. Emotional integrity and system fit

- **EMOTIONAL STAKES ARE EARNED:** relationships gain importance through shared history and action, not sudden tragedy.
- **AFTERMATH SPACE:** after significant loss or relief, leave room for players to choose reactions.
- **SACRIFICE PAYOFF:** voluntary cost must have plausible benefit and consequence; no forced heroic death.
- **EMOTIONAL CONTRAST / RECOVERY:** alternate pressure with breathing space naturally when the fiction allows it.
- **SYSTEM-FIT BEFORE HEAVY BENDING:** if a playstyle needs constant rule overrides, compare systems or adopt a transparent overlay.
- **SOURCE / TABLE KNOWLEDGE PROVENANCE:** distinguish official rules from table lore, social agreement and private source content.


## Source-fidelity supplements — distinct pattern boundaries (RC-EN-0.5)

This section keeps a one-to-one source-label audit for selected concepts that the earlier compact English index merged, renamed or dropped. Labels in backticks are upstream identifiers, not player-visible menu prompts. Apply only the entry relevant to the current problem.

- **CLASSIFICATION:** Source label `CLASSIFICAZIONE`. CORE CANDIDATE denotes a near-universal invariant; PROTOCOL needs a specific trigger; MODIFIER changes an authorized procedure; LIBRARY ONLY stays optional. Being in this index does not automatically promote a pattern to CORE.
- **ACTIVE PREMISE LEGIBILITY — ALEXANDRIAN P0 REFINEMENT:** A scenario pitch should expose an actionable opening rather than merely describing mood. Offer agency without prescribing the correct choice.
- **FUNCTION-PRESERVING SCENARIO TRANSPOSITION — LIBRARY ONLY:** A situation may be adapted to a new genre or location only if the real decision structure, consequences, time and actor incentives still make sense. The presentation can change while core causality remains.
- **FICTIONAL DECEPTION ≠ FACILITATOR DECEPTION:** NPCs, rumors and in-world documents can lie. The facilitator cannot fabricate rules, dice, sources, established canon or false player causality merely to create a surprising plot twist. If a constraint is predetermined by the contract, communicate its scope instead of pretending a player caused it.
- **FAIL-FORWARD SCOPE / RETRY ≠ RESET:** Not a universal rule: it applies only when the system/source/table permits it. When active, failure changes state; retry starts from that changed fiction rather than resetting the same path automatically.
- **REVELATION ≠ NAVIGATION — ALEXANDRIAN P0 REFINEMENT:** In a mystery, test two separate graphs: what evidence establishes a proposition, and what lead lets the characters reach the next actionable node. A clue pointing to a location is not automatically proof of a conclusion; a discovered fact need not itself tell the PCs where to go. Critical chokepoints need plausible independent routes.
- **SPEECH ≠ COMPREHENSION:** Record what an NPC says, language/register, what the PC can actually understand and what the player's interface translates. Do not make every fictional language automatically intelligible because the facilitator narrates in English.
- **STAT-BLOCK → BEHAVIOR COMPILER:** Translate available combat features into tactics from an actor's goals, knowledge, terrain, risk and morale; never optimize enemies with omniscient awareness of player character resources.
- **LIFE-SKILL THEME BANK — EXAMPLES:** Upstream label `LIFE-SKILL THEME BANK — ESEMPI`. Examples include informed judgment, keeping promises, responsibility, recovery after failure, negotiating conflict, seeing multiple viewpoints and recognizing manipulation. Use these only as optional fiction-friendly themes; never force therapy, lessons or a predetermined moral conclusion.
- **LOCAL HOPE / DURABLE VICTORY — LIBRARY ONLY:** In a consensually bleak campaign, allow at least some plausible local objectives the characters can affect. Preserve earned victories proportionately without promising survival or overriding the agreed dark tone. New pressure should follow causally from the world, not automatically erase every success.
- **VALUED EXPERIENCE ≠ POSITIVE AFFECT:** Sadness, tension, grief, surprise or regret can be part of a positively valued story. Do not infer success from smiles alone, or claim that tears, intense emotion or shock automatically prove the game was good. Ask participants about fun and voluntary desire to return independently.
- **PLAYSTYLE PROFILE ≠ RULESET:** Labels such as cinematic, OSR, investigative, sandbox or tactical express aims, not auto-enabled rules. Keep active source/edition, table procedure, scenario assumption and house rule explicit; consider system mismatch before silently changing many mechanics.

## Q. V0.4 candidate state, continuity and learning

These are **RESEARCH CANDIDATES, NOT VALIDATED CAPABILITIES**. Use them only when the complete reviewed runtime chain exists and they do not conflict with CORE, source rules, role ownership or player knowledge.

- **STATE COMPOSITION / CROSS-THREAD RECOMBINATION:** established threads may interact if causally relevant; no retroactive central conspiracy.
- **CHARACTER CONCEPT EVOLUTION:** actions may change reputation and external world reactions; players own internal identity.
- **EMERGENT SALIENCE PROMOTION:** sustained table attention can increase an element's priority without making it immortal or automatically targeted.
- **RELEVANCE-TRIGGERED THREAD RECAP:** recall only player-known established facts when an old thread becomes actionable.
- **SUSTAINED SPLIT-PARTY:** maintain each branch's time, position, resources and knowledge; do not force reconvergence.
- **THREAT-MODEL LEARNING:** characters and adversaries learn only from plausible observations.
- **PERSISTENT RELATIONAL STATE:** betrayal or trust can have layered consequences without imposing emotions.
- **SUSPICION DEBT:** avoid teaching players to repeatedly check every harmless object through unsupported hidden traps.
- **CALLBACK SCOPE TYPING:** TABLE, CHARACTER, CAMPAIGN and WORLD memories are not interchangeable.
- **TEMPORARY SIDECAST:** a brief side POV may support a playable consequence without taking over another PC.

Four **supplemental, research-only** topics: taught behavior and later companion reapplication; transformed character continuity versus social recognition; coordination via legible shared intent; diegetic cast turnover with fast re-entry. Consult `V0.4-CANDIDATE.md` for exact operational restrictions.
