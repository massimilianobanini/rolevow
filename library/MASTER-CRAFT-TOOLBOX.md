## Purpose and use contract

This is an **on-demand toolbox for human Game Masters**, not a second CORE, not a mandatory checklist and not an independent ruleset. Identify the GM's actual problem, active system and edition, group experience, available time and desired output. Retrieve only **one to three** relevant techniques; describe **when**, **what to do now**, the **trade-off/risk**, and an observable sign of improvement. Prefer lower-prep options when effectiveness is comparable; if the GM enjoys elaborate prep, that preference can justify it. For exact mechanics consult the 2014, 2024 or Daggerheart adapter and source.

## 1. NPCs, factions and antagonists

- **MINIMUM VIABLE NPC / PROGRESSIVE ELABORATION:** Begin with function, one recognizable cue, desire and what this NPC can actually know. Elaborate only after recurrence or real player interest. Risk: disposable caricatures if the cue replaces motivation.
- **SIGNATURE CUE CONTINUITY:** Reuse distinctive gesture, voice, clothing or habit sparingly so people recognize the NPC; a signature is a retrieval cue, not a compulsory gimmick.
- **LATENT NPC POOL → PROMOTION:** Keep unnamed guards/crew/townspeople in a generic pool; promote a relevant individual to persistent state with id, appearance, desire and relationship.
- **REUSABLE ROLE SHELL / CAUSAL RESKIN:** Reuse an archetypal function across contexts only when geography, role and resources make that appearance plausible; reuse structure, not a teleported identical person.
- **NPC DIEGETIC KNOWLEDGE / GOALS / SELF-PRESERVATION:** NPCs act from plausible information, incentives, fear and resources. They do not automatically know HP, secrets or future GM plans; retreat and surrender are possible.
- **LIVING NPC / FACTION PRESSURE:** Give important organizations a plausible next move, means and timing. Let the world advance off-screen without making player interference meaningless.
- **PRESSURE CHANNELS:** Choose one or two coherent pressures—institutions, social standing, opportunity denial, alliances, deals—rather than random escalating attacks.
- **RECURRING VILLAIN ≠ PLOT ARMOR:** If the party legitimately defeats or persuades the villain, let it happen; unearned escapes and immunity undermine player agency.
- **FICTIONAL PRESSURE, NOT REAL-PERSON TARGETING:** Stakes may threaten fictional promises, reputations, relationships and resources. Do not exploit sensitive characteristics of a real participant for a desired emotional reaction.

## 2. Efficient prep, reuse and improvisation

- **PREP VALUE DENSITY / SYSTEM-DEPENDENT PREP FLOOR:** Prioritize high-likelihood, high-impact, reusable content. Respect the minimum preparation necessary to run the actual system accurately.
- **NEXT-SESSION INTENT → FOCUSED PREP:** Use the players' stated next goal to prepare likely situations deeply and other branches lightly; the world can still move.
- **UNUSED PREP IS INVENTORY, NOT FAILURE:** Store modular unused content; never force a prepared scene into play to justify its creation.
- **PREP SUNK COST ≠ PROTECTION:** Time spent writing a boss or twist is not a reason to negate a valid player solution.
- **REUSE MODE / PROMISE DEBT:** Separate a recurring canonical NPC, a reskinned template, a shared-world appearance and an easter egg; repeated mysterious appearances create real narrative expectations.
- **RELEVANCE ≠ TRUTH:** Player curiosity can make a thing important without making their theory automatically true. Differentiate FIXED CANON from genuinely OPEN facts.
- **CONSENSUAL CANONIZATION:** Player proposals become world facts only after the table's real co-creation permission and acceptance.
- **IMPROVISATION PAUSE / CANON-DEBT FIREWALL:** A short pause to check current canon, actor motives and open uncertainty is better than an elaborate contradictory invention.
- **RESKIN / REFLAVOR WITH MECHANICAL FIREWALL:** Change presentation freely when mechanics remain legal; mechanical changes require an identified house rule or source-authorized variant.
- **MODULE PREP AS DIFF / SITUATION VIEW:** For a legitimately available published adventure, record only modifications, impacted clues and actor states instead of rewriting the module or scripting every scene.
- **SCENARIO STRUCTURE FIT / ACTIVE PREMISE:** Match the scenario to actual play activities—investigation, heist, travel, politics, dungeon—and offer an actionable opening without forcing a particular solution.
- **PREP DELIGHT ≠ PREP OBLIGATION:** Enjoyable map-making or worldbuilding may be worth doing, but label it optional hobby time rather than mandatory prep.

## 3. Agency, consequences and scenario structure

- **STRONG START, OUTCOME OPEN:** Put the party near a decision with stakes; never predetermine the resolution.
- **SCRIPTED SETBACK ≠ EARNED CONSEQUENCE:** Capture, theft and loss must not occur regardless of an intervention that could legitimately prevent them.
- **FALSE CHOICE / CAUSAL ATTRIBUTION:** Before offering A/B, know what could really change. Do not blame a fixed event on a choice that did not affect it.
- **LINEARITY ≠ RAILROAD:** A transparent linear premise can still permit meaningful choices in approach, cost, alliances and aftermath.
- **ACTIVE OPPOSITION / WORLD ADVANCE:** Threats act while the party does other things, but valid prevention must change outcomes; the GM's notes are not fate.
- **BOTTLENECK BEFORE RESCUER:** Fix clues dependent on a single roll, gate or NPC before inserting a last-minute rescuer.
- **TRAVEL COMPANION AS NUDGE, NOT SOLVER:** Companions can remind or prompt; they must not take the party's key discoveries or decisions.
- **SIDE THREAD VALUE TEST:** A side quest may stand alone if it produces choice, character, relief, resources, relationships or consequences; repeated player interest can legitimately change the campaign's center.
- **PERSONAL HOOK → PARTY BRIDGE:** Give other PCs a plausible shared reason to engage with a personal backstory hook.
- **REAL DEADLINE → REAL TRADE-OFF:** A countdown that never changes when the PCs delay is fake urgency.
- **NEGATIVE SPACE IS VALID CANON:** An empty room may stay empty. Searching does not create loot or traps simply because the player tried.
- **CAMPAIGN SCOPE PIVOT IS NEGOTIATED:** If play grows from local adventuring to faction/domain play, discuss the new campaign contract rather than forcing old rails.

## 4. Rolls, competence, rules and procedures

- **UNCERTAINTY BEFORE ROLL:** A meaningful roll requires uncertainty and a relevant possible consequence; never roll for an already certain result.
- **CREATIVE ACTION → ADJUDICATION PATH:** Translate the player's real goal and approach into a lawful procedure, stakes and outcome; creativity is neither automatically impossible nor automatically successful.
- **COMPETENCE INTEGRITY:** Do not secretly increase DC/Difficulty to cancel a character's actual expertise.
- **RESOURCE-SPEND CAUSALITY:** An ability or slot spent should have its lawful opportunity; it does not guarantee success, but cannot be nullified solely to protect the plot.
- **PRE-COMBAT LEVERAGE PERSISTS:** Scouting, positioning and sabotage before a confrontation must remain relevant after combat begins.
- **PROCEDURE INTEGRITY:** When time, exploration, reactions or encounter turns matter under the active system, use that system's real steps and resource timing.
- **ACTIONABLE-FIRST RULE LOOKUP:** Offer a brief practical answer now, then exact source and caveat when required; label RAW, optional rule, table variant and provisional ruling.
- **SYSTEM STUDY → TABLE CUSTOMIZATION:** To learn the system, test a source-faithful baseline first; for table customization, negotiate and track each override explicitly.
- **PEER-TAUGHT FLUENCY ≠ SOURCE CORRECTNESS:** A familiar house interpretation is not automatically the official rule; verify consequential disputed mechanics.
- **FAIL-FORWARD SCOPE / RETRY ≠ RESET:** Apply fail-forward only when authorized by rules or the table. Failure changes state; another attempt begins from that changed state.
- **FAILURE-INVERSION FIREWALL:** A failed investigation check does not grant meta-information by suggesting the opposite of a secret.
- **ROBUST ESSENTIAL CLUES:** Critical information needs multiple plausible acquisition paths when a single failed check would otherwise halt the scenario.

## 5. Puzzles, props and information representation

- **CHALLENGE LOCUS:** Clarify whether a puzzle tests the human player, the fictional PC, or both; character competence can provide legitimate hints and bypasses.
- **SOCIAL INTENT / APPROACH / CHARACTER COMPETENCE:** Separate what the PC wants, the player's leverage and the system-defined execution. Roleplaying is not a public speaking competition.
- **MULTI-STAGE CIPHER:** Honor an early solved puzzle; do not add a surprise second cipher just to extend play unless it was already fictionally grounded.
- **TOOLBOX, NOT HIERARCHY:** Grid, zones, theatre of mind, VTT, paper cards and improvised props should be chosen by decision value and friction—not prestige.
- **EXTERNALIZED STATE / SINGLE SOURCE OF TRUTH:** Use simple trackers and determine which record is authoritative; reconcile conflicting notes instead of silently selecting convenient facts.

## 6. Dynamic combat without cheating

- **COMBAT REPRESENTATION MODE:** Select grid, zones, theatre of mind or hybrid based on the specific system's precision requirements and the group's needs.
- **BATTLEFIELD AFFORDANCE BUDGET:** Provide a few distinct actionable terrain features, not pages of decoration.
- **SECONDARY OBJECTIVE / DYNAMIC AXIS:** Protection, escape, countdowns and control points may matter more than eliminating enemy HP; make objectives legible before commitment.
- **ENEMY PURPOSE BEFORE TACTICS:** Actors have goals and survival instincts; tactics derive from them, not from GM omniscience.
- **PLAYER NARRATION HANDOFF:** Let players describe a success's appearance if welcomed, without giving unilateral authority to alter mechanics.
- **TURN PACING WITHOUT PUNISHMENT:** Reduce waiting through clear status and prep, not secret penalties or unagreed timers.
- **STAGNATION ESCAPE HATCH:** When repeated rounds add no meaningful state changes, examine morale, retreat, objectives or source-legal compression.
- **BOSS SHOCK RESULT FIREWALL:** Boss immunity, new stages and extra health cannot be added retroactively after surprising legitimate damage.
- **FUNCTIONAL TOKENS > PRODUCTION VALUE:** Readable tokens can be as useful as expensive miniatures.
- **VISIBLE UNCERTAINTY AS DRAMA:** Openly shown rolls may create suspense if that choice matches table expectations; do not change visibility just to manipulate an outcome.
- **SYSTEM-FIT BEFORE HEAVY BENDING:** If the desired playstyle constantly requires overriding the rules, consider a different game or a transparent variant.
- **PLAYSTYLE PROFILE ≠ RULESET:** Cinematic/OSR/narrative/tactical describes a goal, not a universal mechanical import.

## 7. Armies, hordes and scaling

- **SCALE / ZOOM ABSTRACTION:** Aggregate state when useful without duplicating or erasing real resources and objectives.
- **MASS-CONFLICT MODE SELECTOR:** Choose background war, player-influenced conflict or fully played conflict based on meaningful investments and decisions.
- **ABSTRACTION → TRADE-OFF:** Compressed hours of action still need choices about resources, priorities and danger, not automatic arbitrary losses.

## 8. Table feedback, consent and fit

- **BEHAVIOR → IMPACT → REQUEST:** Address observable behavior and effects, then one actionable request; avoid personality diagnoses.
- **SEVERITY-BASED TIMING:** Small pacing issues can wait; harassment, safety boundaries and non-consensual PvP may require immediate pause.
- **CONSENT WITHOUT SPOILER:** Agree broad PvP/betrayal boundaries without exposing every future twist; everyone involved needs meaningful consent.
- **SCAFFOLD THEN FADE:** Give beginners examples and gradually reduce prompting as they gain autonomy.
- **TABLE-FIT ESCAPE HATCH:** Some table preferences are incompatible; changing group, system or format can be more useful than endless negotiation.

## 9. Campaign setup: pitch, Session Zero and onboarding

- **CAMPAIGN CONTRACT STACK:** Distinguish pitch/intention, collaborative Session Zero, character/rules onboarding, pilot play and later recalibration.
- **PITCH = INFORMED OFFER:** State system, premise, tone, expected agency, schedule, effort and non-negotiable limits before participation.
- **SESSION ZERO = COLLABORATIVE NEGOTIATION:** Discuss safety, PvP, secrecy, table style, logistics, feedback and party cohesion after people opt in.
- **ONBOARDING ≠ SESSION ZERO:** Learning mechanics and making characters takes actual time, but is a separate need.
- **LIVING CAMPAIGN CHARTER:** Keep a brief editable record of agreements.
- **DECLARED FIT ≠ OBSERVED FIT:** A short pilot can reveal mismatches between preferences and play.
- **MID-CAMPAIGN RECALIBRATION:** Reopen table contracts when reality changes.
- **PARTY COHESION BY DESIGN:** Give PCs credible reasons to collaborate, without forcing long backstories.

## 10. One-shots and time-boxed sessions

- **TRANSPARENT PREMISE CONTRACT:** Declare the constrained mission upfront; choices about its resolution remain real.
- **ENDING RESERVE / CLOSURE BUDGET:** Preserve a window for consequences and closure; cut expendable content first.
- **ELASTIC CONTENT — MUST / CUTTABLE / BUFFER:** Design minimum playable core, removable extras and optional filler separately.
- **START NEAR THE FIRST MEANINGFUL DECISION:** Skip logistics that do not change the scenario.
- **ONBOARDING COUNTS AGAINST THE CLOCK:** Character creation and rules explanations consume the same finite session time.
- **REAL-TIME CLOCK AS OPTIONAL DRAMA:** Time pressure needs table consent and credible fictional effects.
- **ONE-SHOT AS PILOT / PROLOGUE:** It can test the campaign concept and still end as a complete local story.

## 11. Incapacitation, death, absence and continuity

- **INCAPACITY VIEW MODE:** Decide whether a player temporarily observes outside their PC, sees only PC-known content or fades to recovery. Keep PLAYER_KNOWN separate from PC_KNOWN.
- **DEATH / LEGACY CLOSURE:** Honor source rules, consequences and a possible replacement PC without forced spectacle or inherited secrets.
- **ABSENCE / TIME-SKIP CAUSALITY:** The world changes while PCs are absent, according to existing actor motives and clocks.
- **CAMPAIGN ANCHOR ≠ SINGLE PC:** A group, institution, location or world can carry continuity after a protagonist leaves.
- **CAMPAIGN CONTINUITY LOCUS:** Track whether persistent memory belongs to a character, party, mission/front, region/world or a hybrid; character loss need not erase campaign state.


## Source-fidelity supplements — explicit boundaries (RC-EN-0.5)

These entries reconcile precise upstream labels that were abbreviated, renamed, or omitted in the earlier compact English toolbox. They remain optional on-demand techniques; they do not replace CORE, protocols or active rules.

- **MODULE PREP AS DIFF / SITUATION VIEW — ALEXANDRIAN P1:** When the published adventure is legally available, use it as the baseline and record only changes with a location/actor anchor, cause, effect on clues, access and balance. Retrieve the relevant actors, locations and information for the current situation—not a forced sequence of scenes. If the adaptation becomes too extensive, declare a local replacement; do not call it an exact original-source reproduction.
- **SCENARIO STRUCTURE FIT / ACTIVE PREMISE — ALEXANDRIAN P0/P1:** The opening must make at least one real opportunity to act perceptible. Fit an investigation, negotiation, heist, dungeon or exploration to the task participants actually agreed to. Offer legible hooks to unsure players without steering proactive players back to a scripted solution.
- **FALSE CHOICE / CAUSAL ATTRIBUTION GUARD:** Before presenting alternatives, know what could genuinely differ. A transparent linear premise is possible; pretending a predetermined outcome was caused by a player's action is not.
- **SYSTEM STUDY MODE → TABLE CUSTOMIZATION MODE:** If the objective is to evaluate a ruleset, start with a source-faithful baseline and log deviations separately. If the goal is a specific group's enjoyment, table-approved house rules may be used—but with explicit scope, not silent imports.
- **CAMPAIGN PITCH = INFORMED OFFER:** Upstream label `DICHIARAZIONE DI INTENTI = OFFERTA INFORMATA`. Before participation, communicate game/edition, premise, expected agency, tone, duration, scheduling and important non-negotiable house rules.
- **SESSION ZERO = COLLABORATIVE NEGOTIATION:** Upstream label `SESSIONE ZERO = NEGOZIAZIONE COLLABORATIVA`. With players who have opted in, agree limits, PvP/secrets, preferred style, logistics, feedback, absences, character ties and negotiable rules.
- **ONBOARDING ≠ SESSION ZERO:** Teaching the system, choosing a character and showing the interface are onboarding tasks that use time, even when hosted at the same meeting; they are not substitutes for consent or a shared social contract.
- **ELASTIC CONTENT BANDS — MUST / CUTTABLE / BUFFER:** MUST produces a coherent complete session; CUTTABLE adds material that can be removed without damaging earned consequences; BUFFER is only for spare time. Do not cut a player's already earned results to satisfy an arbitrary clock.
- **REAL-TIME CLOCK AS OPTIONAL DRAMATIC MECHANIC:** A real timer can represent an in-fiction deadline only with transparent consent and a causal consequence. Never use it as a hidden penalty or a universal speed enforcement tool.

## Final decision rule

A technique is useful only if it solves the human GM's actual problem with an acceptable prep/accuracy/agency/safety trade-off. Retrieve **one to three techniques**, adapt to the active ruleset, and give an immediately usable result before theory. The Italian library remains the canonical source if semantic discrepancies emerge during review; report them rather than silently overriding it.
