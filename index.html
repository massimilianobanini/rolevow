01 / THE PROBLEM

You want to play. Life doesn't always cooperate.

A tabletop RPG may have an amazing world and great rules. Yet the game may never start, or take more work than you want.

01

You want to play. No group is available.

Schedules clash, there is no GM, or you simply want an adventure when you have time.

02

You're the GM. Preparation takes your whole evening.

Ideas, rules, NPCs, pacing and different player interests: not all the work you do reaches the table.

03

Any AI can tell a story. Can it help you play?

A great description is not enough if the story ignores your choices, forgets facts or fails to support the game.

The point is not to produce more material. It is to create an experience worth people's time.

08 / FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Answers before you begin.

If you cannot find what you need, consult the public documents or ask RoleVow.

Is RoleVow really free?

Yes. No RoleVow subscription, credits or tokens are required. RoleVow runs on ChatGPT, which has its own plans and limits. This page does not request payment details.

Do turns or Game Master requests consume credits?

RoleVow does not sell credits or turn bundles or charge for each decision, scene or GM question. ChatGPT may have different limits by account, model, tools and extended use. Unlimited, uninterrupted play is not promised. See the official ChatGPT Free Tier FAQ.

Can I continue a campaign if a chat becomes too long?

RoleVow provides for continuity checkpoints. Before stopping, ask for a gameplay checkpoint and save the text; in a new conversation provide it and ask to resume. This is neither automatic nor perfect: check inventory, decisions and the last scene. Checkpoint guide ↗.

What does “Simple Tabletop RPG Tool” mean? Is it a new game?

No. RoleVow is a tool for existing tabletop RPGs, not a new game or a simplified ruleset. Simple means less friction when starting, playing, preparing and resuming a campaign, while keeping your chosen rules and creativity.

Do I need to know D&D or write prompts?

No. After starting, choose Play Now and delegate initial choices, or customize progressively. AI may still make mistakes; verify sources when a rule matters.

Does RoleVow replace a human Game Master?

Not necessarily. It can run a chat adventure when you have no GM, or be a second pair of eyes for an existing GM. If your table already works well without AI, nothing needs replacing.

Won't AI take the fun out of preparation?

For many GMs, inventing stories, encounters and maps is part of the hobby. You do not have to delegate it. Decide when a second opinion helps, without giving up what you enjoy. Why RoleVow exists ↗.

Why not use ordinary ChatGPT or my random tables?

You can. RoleVow combines guided paths, preferences, rules procedures and continuity without requiring complex prompts. General superiority over a well-configured chatbot or a good text file has not been demonstrated. Explore use cases ↗.

Why play with AI instead of a video game?

They are different experiences. In chat, you can propose actions freely in natural language and ask for them to be resolved under a tabletop RPG system. You may not have the graphics, immediacy or social experience of another medium. It is especially useful to try when no GM or group is available.

What exactly is the negative price?

RoleVow costs €0. After actual use, an eligible dissatisfied adult may request a symbolic €1 net reputational indemnity, subject to the terms and available funding. It is not automatic. Official terms ↗.

How do I request the euro if I am dissatisfied?

After genuinely using RoleVow, ask it in your ChatGPT conversation how to leave feedback or request the indemnity. It will provide the official English form. Select the indemnity option, confirm eligibility and supply proportionate evidence and a preferred payment route. In hosted groups only the human chat host may claim; other participants may give feedback. The operator reviews requests manually and aims to respond within seven calendar days, not a fixed payout deadline. Conditions and eligibility ↗.

How do I get the most out of RoleVow?

Start simply, then say what you enjoy, what you want to avoid and what you wish to do yourself, such as rolling dice, inventing plots or choosing character actions. More reasoning effort may help with complex rules, but can be slower and does not guarantee accuracy. For long campaigns use checkpoints. Complete practical guide ↗.

Can I save a campaign in Google Drive without losing details or getting spoilers?

You can keep versioned checkpoints. Drive is optional; connecting an account alone does not update files automatically, and summaries may omit details. Players must not have access to GM-only notes; a label asking the AI not to reveal secrets is not technical access control. Instructions and precautions ↗.

Can I add images during play?

Yes, if your ChatGPT account and conversation allow image generation. You can request scenes, environments, characters and important objects. Images are optional and may slow the pace; RoleVow starts with text to reduce friction. Ask for images when they add value.

Can I use RoleVow with Gemini or Claude?

The current public test is designed for ChatGPT. Gemini and Claude are not declared supported. Current limitations ↗.

09 / THE ROLEVOW MANIFESTO

Fun is the result. Everything else is a tool.

A good ruleset, an amazing adventure and even a capable AI are not enough if people at the table are not having fun.

RoleVow follows 11 principles. Here they are, without making you open another document.

  1. 01

    RPGs exist for people, not for material.

    The result matters more than the number of pages produced: it is the experience of the people playing.

  2. 02

    Fun is not assumed.

    Observe, listen and check what worked, then improve what did not.

  3. 03

    Personalize before standardizing.

    Different tables and people have different needs. The method must adapt.

  4. 04

    Different ways to play. The same final question.

    In person, online or in chat: did people have fun?

  5. 05

    AI is a means, not the goal.

    It should remove practical obstacles, not replace people's creativity.

  6. 06

    Depth when you want it. Simplicity at the start.

    Those who want more detail can explore it. Those who want to play now need not study first.

  7. 07

    Less work that does not create fun.

    Especially for GMs: less wasted preparation, more value at the table.

  8. 08

    Real freedom, real consequences.

    Players' choices must be able to change the world, including when they make mistakes.

  9. 09

    Failure is information.

    An unsuccessful experience provides an opportunity to correct and test again.

  10. 10

    The risk should not be yours alone.

    Within its terms, the Ultra-Guarantee gives a real cost to an unmet promise.

  11. 11

    Open does not mean finished.

    The project states its limitations and must improve through real use and testing.

Our north star: maximize people's real enjoyment and their voluntary desire to play again.
