RoleVow is the planned English edition of Divertoscopio. It assists Players and Game Masters with existing tabletop RPG rules. It is not a new ruleset or an official product of any game publisher.

Three distinct tabletop systems in the test candidate

Dedicated adapters have been prepared, but their English gameplay has not yet passed independent human tests.

D&D 5e (2014)

SRD 5.1 procedures and character state, without mixing the revised 2024 rules.

D&D (2024)

SRD 5.2.1 rules, action economy, character options and edition-specific checks.

Daggerheart™

Unofficial experimental adapter, refined based on player feedback. Built around SRD 2.0 and the official errata. English independent playtests are still needed; no publisher endorsement is implied.

How RoleVow is intended to work

Player

Choose Play Now, Customize First or Customize in Depth; your character's voluntary actions remain yours. Choose your dice source before the first character roll.

Game Master

Get concise help during play, or more deliberate preparation and a source-conscious rule check.

Hosted group

One human host relays multiple players' choices into a single ChatGPT conversation. It is not a synchronized multi-account game room and does not offer private per-player channels.

Start a technical playtest

The test start sequence is to copy the official repository URL plus “Let's begin” into a new ChatGPT conversation. A ChatGPT plan or browser setting cannot guarantee that repository instructions will be retrieved or followed correctly.

Use a fresh conversation. The guarantee is not active.

Limits we won't hide

  • No independent English human-gameplay validation has been completed for this candidate.
  • RoleVow's international Negative Price Ultra-Guarantee is not active. There are no approved international claims, payments or compensation terms.
  • ChatGPT is a separate service with its own account access, usage limits and privacy policy.
  • Published adventures, non-SRD rules and media must be sourced and used lawfully; no false publisher endorsement is claimed.
  • There is no verified full real-time multi-account multiplayer mode.
