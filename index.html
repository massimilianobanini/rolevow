Frequently asked questions

Do I need to pay for RoleVow?

No. RoleVow does not sell its own subscriptions or credits. ChatGPT is a separate service with its own plans, fees and limits.

Do I need tabletop RPG experience?

No. You can start with beginner-friendly explanations or customize the experience if you already know the rules.

Does RoleVow replace my Game Master or group?

No. Use it when AI is helpful to you. In hosted group play, one human host relays choices in a single conversation; there are no synchronized individual player accounts.

Is this a finished product?

RoleVow is currently a public technical playtest. Independent English gameplay and long-campaign reliability have not yet been established. AI can make mistakes. Current limitations. Our brand positioning is not independently verified evidence of worldwide priority.
