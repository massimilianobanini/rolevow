# RoleVow — Feedback and Negative Price Ultra-Guarantee privacy notice
**RV-PRIVACY-1.0 — Applies from the opening of the official feedback form.**

This notice applies to feedback and reputational indemnity requests collected through the official RoleVow form. Do not submit personal information or payment data in public GitHub issues.

## Controller and contact
Massimiliano Banini, Italy, is the operator and intended controller of the information submitted directly to RoleVow. Contact: massimiliano.banini@yahoo.it. Additional controller/trader identification and address will be provided where required by applicable law before activation.

## What we collect and why
Ordinary feedback: enjoyment (0–10), desire to return (0–10), optional comments, indemnity request choice and submission timestamp/identifier. The feedback path does not ask for name, email, payment details or chat evidence. Avoid including identifying information in comments. The proposed lawful basis is legitimate interests in understanding and improving the tool, subject to the controller's documented balancing assessment (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

Reputational indemnity request: additionally, full name, adult declaration, solo/host role, country of residence and receipt where applicable, contact email, preferred payout method, redacted evidence of real use and terms acknowledgement. If necessary for an approved payment, limited transfer information is requested separately. We use the information to review requests, communicate decisions, arrange and reconcile transfers, comply with applicable bookkeeping duties and handle disputes. The proposed bases are steps requested by you and performance of the applicable commitment (Art. 6(1)(b)), applicable legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c)), and proportionate legitimate interests in preventing duplicate payments and maintaining accurate records (Art. 6(1)(f)). No marketing opt-in is needed. You can decline to provide claim details; in that event we may be unable to verify or pay the claim, but ordinary feedback and RoleVow use remain available. We make no solely automated eligibility or payment decisions.

## Duplicate prevention and access
The owner checks prior or pending indemnity requests and payments across RoleVow and Divertoscopio; the proposed method is a limited private matching register using a stable pseudonymous key and necessary verified identifiers, with manual handling of ambiguous matches. A pseudonymous key is still personal data if it can be linked to an individual. We do not publish evidence, identities, emails, account details, receipts or identity keys. Please redact all third-party and unrelated information from any excerpt. Use an appropriately restricted link or redacted text rather than an entire account history.

## Providers and transfers
Google Forms, Sheets, Drive and Apps Script handle intake and the private register; email providers carry operational messages and the selected bank or payment service handles necessary payment data. Access is limited to the operator and any necessary authorised service providers. GitHub and OpenAI handle use of their own services under their own policies; RoleVow does not automatically receive ChatGPT transcripts. The controller must verify the actual service contracts, roles, countries and safeguards before activation, including whether transfers outside the EEA occur, the relevant adequacy decision or other applicable safeguards, and how a copy can be obtained. No claim data is published to GitHub. The operator alert contains no claimant identity or evidence.

## Retention schedule
Feedback: retain identifiable/raw responses for no more than 12 months, then delete or irreversibly anonymise where practicable. Unsuccessful/withdrawn claims: delete unnecessary identity, contact and evidence after 90 days from resolution, unless necessary for a dispute or legal obligation. Successful claims: delete unnecessary evidence and operational contact data after 90 days from settlement, subject to disputes and legal obligations; keep the minimum financial record for the applicable statutory period. Keep the minimum necessary cross-program matching record for the duration of the one-payment rule and review necessity at least annually; delete when no longer necessary, subject to applicable duties. Apply deletion to Google Forms raw responses, Sheets, email and evidence copies; provider backups follow applicable verified provider policies. These are the programme's operational periods, subject to applicable statutory obligations. Restrict access, enable account security and review retention regularly.

Depending on the circumstances, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or object to processing. You can contact the controller at the address above. Responses will follow GDPR time limits. You may complain to your competent data-protection authority, including the Italian Garante: https://www.garanteprivacy.it/diritti/come-agire-per-tutelare-i-tuoi-dati-personali/reclamo. No public release of identifiable claim data or evidence is required to request the indemnity.
