The monetary programme is not accepting applications. This page describes the intended terms and is not a currently available offer. Do not submit claim or payment data until an effective date and the official active form are announced.

## What the guarantee means
RoleVow is free to use. ChatGPT and other independent services may have their own fees and limits. When the monetary programme is active, if you genuinely use RoleVow and are dissatisfied, you may apply for a symbolic €1 net reputational indemnity. The indemnity makes the operator financially accountable for genuine dissatisfaction: an extreme form of risk reversal. It is a gesture of reputational accountability, not a reward for participation or reviews. No purchase, subscription, public review or marketing consent is required. Enjoyment cannot be guaranteed.

## Who can request the indemnity
You must be at least 18, have actually used RoleVow during the announced active period, and be personally dissatisfied. In group play only the human host who manages the RoleVow chat may claim; other participants may submit ordinary feedback. One payment per natural person across RoleVow and Divertoscopio combined. An outstanding claim in either programme will be checked before any further reservation. Your subjective dissatisfaction is not graded or rejected merely because other players enjoyed the game.

## How to request it
Use the official English feedback form while claims are open. Select the indemnity request option and provide the requested contact, age, role, country, preferred transfer method and proportionate evidence of actual use. A redacted excerpt or equivalent evidence is acceptable. Do not send private player details, identity documents, bank credentials or unrelated chat transcripts. Initial submission is not a payment approval. The operator reviews completed requests in chronological order without favouring easier or later claims. We aim to provide a first response within seven calendar days; this is a service target, not a fixed payment deadline. If more information is necessary, we will explain what is needed and why.

## Payment reach and costs
The indemnity requires a lawful, workable transfer route capable of delivering €1 net, with a verified maximum all-in cost. Before relying on the indemnity, ask the operator whether a route is available for your country and receiving account. A euro bank transfer is the preferred initial route, where confirmed to work. PayPal or other lawful routes are considered only after checking provider terms, applicable minimums, fees and currency conversion. We will not ask you to pay a fee to make a claim. The approved amount will not be reduced to recover transfer charges. Additional account information, if needed, is requested separately via a suitable private channel.

## Budget and queue
The programme has a €20 gross calendar-month spending ceiling and a €100 gross lifetime ceiling, measured in Europe/Rome time. Both amounts include the €1 net payment plus all applicable sender costs, recipient deductions and currency-conversion costs. The available lifetime budget equals €100 minus gross expenditure already paid and binding gross cost reservations. Beginning a game does not reserve funds. A completed submission establishes review priority; following verification of all conditions and available funding, the operator records the full conservative gross reservation and communicates the decision and proposed payment month. These internal steps do not override any mandatory rights that arise under applicable law. Earlier completed requests will be considered before later ones. If the current month is full but lifetime funding remains, an approved request may be queued. We communicate the expected payment month and any material delay. Unexpected fees or transfer failures are investigated; valid obligations are not retrospectively cancelled simply because of a cost increase. The operator does not undertake obligations above the announced programme limit except to the extent required by mandatory law.

## Closure and review
Intake may close when funding is fully spent/committed or insufficient for a further minimum payment, or may be suspended for a material technical, fraud, legal or security issue. Closure is prospective: it does not remove valid prior rights. Pending submissions made before closure will be reviewed under the applicable terms. RoleVow remains free. Ordinary feedback can still be sent to the project contact when the form is temporarily closed. Ineligible or incomplete requests receive an explanation or proportionate clarification request. You can ask for a correction or human review. No payment is automatic, and no one is required to publish praise, criticism or a testimonial.
