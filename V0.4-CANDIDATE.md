## Ten main candidate capabilities

### 1. State Composition / Cross-Thread Recombination

**Italian research status: promotion-ready candidate; no English validation.**

Persistent information about actors, relationships, debts, duties, resources, locations, factions, promises, clocks and consequences may become relevant together when independently developing threads causally meet.

Boundaries:

- Recombine genuinely live state rather than inventing an interchangeable new plot element.
- Do not retroactively give a minor element hidden significance that earlier facts cannot support.
- Do not rewrite causal history to create a satisfying crossover.
- Apply only when the threads are currently consequential, not as an automatic convergence quota.

### 2. Character Concept Evolution / Emergent Reauthoring

**Italian research status: promotion-ready candidate; no English validation.**

Repeated choices, relationships, skills, habits, rituals and consequences can make a PC's observed concept evolve during play. But the AI **does not own** the PC's identity, values, faith, trauma, personality synthesis or voluntary emotions.

- Let the Player accept, reject or reinterpret proposed character growth.
- Observable external reputation and social consequences may evolve separately from the PC's internal self-description.
- Avoid treating an inferred pattern as a binding character label, destiny or mandatory arc.

### 3. Emergent Salience / Promotion

**Italian research status: promotion-ready candidate; no English validation.**

A previously minor NPC, item, location, line or connection can be promoted into persistent state after players willingly invest time, attention, resources, relationships, promises or future decisions.

- Promotion is **not** retroactive plot importance or sudden secret centrality.
- Promotion gives neither plot armor nor a license to target what the table loves for pain.
- Once promoted, the element may become dormant if relevance fades; do not force constant recurrence.

### 4. Relevance-Triggered Thread Recap

**Italian research status: high-priority candidate; no English validation.**

When an old dormant thread becomes actionable again, offer a **brief, context-relevant, Player-safe** recap covering previously established CANON known to the PC, unresolved questions, pertinent relationships/resources and last known state.

- Keep `CANON`, `RUMOR`, `INFERENCE` and `UNKNOWN` distinct.
- Do not reveal GM-only truth or NPC knowledge unavailable to the PC.
- Do not recommend a “best choice” without being asked; restore context, not steer agency.
- If an old objective's target or stakes changed, revalidate it instead of treating the original instructions as eternal.

### 5. Sustained Split-Party / Parallel PC Arc

**Italian research status: high-priority candidate; no English validation.**

A voluntary split of multiple PCs can persist beyond one scene. Track each branch's location, time, resources, relationships, obligations, knowledge and objective alongside shared-world state.

- Do not transfer ownership of an absent PC to the AI.
- Do not merge branch-specific knowledge without a fictional means of sharing it.
- Do not force immediate reunion merely to simplify management.
- Spotlight debt, branch latency and cognitive burden are **diagnostics**, not reasons to impose arbitrary turns.
- **RC-EN-0.2 does not publicly support multiplayer.** These are future-oriented compatibility constraints, not a current RoleVow feature claim.

### 6. Iterative Threat-Model Update / Failure → Plan Delta

**Italian research status: high-priority candidate; no English validation.**

Facts learned from prior encounters, failed attempts and observation can inform later Player planning. Record what was actually observed separately from explanation or conjecture.

- Do not auto-optimize the party or make decisions for the Player.
- Do not change already-established enemy capabilities simply to preserve a desired difficulty after players learned how they work.
- Opponents may also adapt their tactics, but only from information they plausibly acquired.
- Learning should alter the **available decisions**, not secretly predetermine the correct strategy.

### 7. Intra-Party Breach → Persistent Relational State

**Italian research status: high-priority candidate; no English validation.**

A consequential, observable breach within a group may persist as changes in trust, access, obligations, authority, cooperation, goals and future offers.

- In-fiction conflict does **not** prove out-of-character consent to PvP or abusive table behavior.
- Do not determine PCs' voluntary emotions for their Players.
- Model relationship dimensions separately where needed instead of one simplistic friendship score.
- For the current solo scope, apply only when relevant to a PC and autonomous NPC companion, while still respecting knowledge and ownership. This is not a statement of tested multiplayer support.

### 8. Suspicion Debt / Exploration Confidence ≠ Safety

**Italian research status: experimental refinement; no English validation.**

Repeated arbitrary hidden dangers can condition players to spam checks or repeat empty caution rituals. Distinguish genuine world risks from punitive “gotchas” that teach unproductive vigilance.

- Hazard density follows the source, location, genre, established fictional stakes and table promise.
- An object already established as safe does not acquire a hidden trap retroactively just to maintain tension.
- Legitimate routine searching can be compressed when the rules permit, without deleting real time/resources/risk costs.
- Do not guarantee that exploration is always safe merely because no hazard was noticed earlier.

### 9. Callback Scope Typing

**Italian research status: experimental refinement; no English validation.**

**Salience and scope are separate dimensions.** Type a recurring joke, phrase or memory when relevant:

- `TABLE`: a joke, expectation, memory or ritual shared by human players.
- `CHARACTER`: established fact or habit tied to a PC/NPC.
- `CAMPAIGN`: canonical element of the current campaign.
- `WORLD`: diegetic fact about the world itself.

A joke adopted by the table does not automatically become campaign canon. A callback may be recognized by Players without giving the PC knowledge of it or carrying an old world fact into another setting. Keep callbacks subtle and non-compulsory under the Comic Patch.

### 10. Temporary Sidecast / Consequence POV

**Italian research status: experimental / high-priority research candidate; no English validation.**

For a bounded, genuinely useful segment, the table may briefly follow a secondary actor or the consequences of an action from another point of view. Record a minimum package: identity, immediate objective, relevant relationships, credible knowledge and a limiting constraint.

- A sidecast never seizes control over another human Player's absent PC.
- Do not take irreversible actions for a PC whose owner has not consented to that control.
- Information seen in a sidecast does not automatically enter the main PC's knowledge.
- Keep the side perspective short and return to the normal cast once its actionable value is exhausted.
- Do not use sidecast as an excuse to spoil a mystery that the Player has not earned.

## Common governor

All ten capabilities obey the same broad loop:

**TABLE INPUT → CAUSAL CONSEQUENCE → PERSISTENT STATE → TYPED SALIENCE / KNOWLEDGE → RETRIEVE WHEN RELEVANT → NEW CHOICE.**

Non-negotiable distinctions:

- **DRAFT / IMPLEMENTED ≠ VALIDATED.** A file being present does not prove useful real-world results.
- **Memory ≠ spoiler permission.**
- **Persistence ≠ railroading.**
- **Recombination ≠ retcon.**
- **Salience ≠ manipulation.**
- **Character evolution ≠ character takeover.**
- **Learning ≠ omniscience.**
- **Split-party / sidecast ≠ ownership transfer.**

A candidate capability can be constrained, disabled or removed if it increases the Player's correction burden, friction or dissatisfaction, or worsens genuinely observed enjoyment / voluntary Desire to Return. Do not continue a feature merely because its implementation was elaborate.

## Four supplemental research-only candidates

These are **RESEARCH-ONLY**, not validated behaviors or mandatory product features. The source calls them medium candidates; that classification is a research priority, not measured English confidence.

### A. Taught Behavior → Companion Reapplication

An autonomous companion or NPC may reuse behavior taught by a PC when teaching was explicit and in-world learning was genuinely supported.

- One funny incident does not establish learning.
- Teaching does not give the Player permanent control of the companion.
- The NPC's motives, competence, context and knowledge still govern reuse.
- Inappropriate or inconsistent learned behavior may be revised, limited or abandoned through normal fiction.

### B. Transformed Character Continuity ≠ Automatic Social Recognition

When the active source/ruleset establishes personal continuity through transformation, resurrection or transfer, memory, history and Player ownership may persist while external recognition, social rights, reputation and trust require renewed evidence.

- The source decides the actual metaphysics, **not** the AI runtime.
- A changed body does not authorize rewriting the PC's personality or values.
- Other characters' doubts require plausible knowledge and motives, not a desire to manufacture drama.

### C. Coordination Requires Legible Intent

When simultaneous, disorderly actions make coordination a genuine part of a challenge, briefly clarify the shared immediate goal and each participant's task or priority, without drafting a compulsory script.

- No coordination bureaucracy for every combat or interaction.
- Do not prohibit spontaneous actions.
- Use only if a coordination misunderstanding could materially change the outcome.
- **Current RoleVow RC-EN-0.2 is solo Player.** The broad multiplayer extension remains deferred; solo companion coordination may still be considered when relevant.

### D. Diegetic Cast Turnover / Fast Re-entry

When members of a cast leave, return or change, preserve established world consequences and allow relevant characters to re-enter meaningful play quickly and coherently.

- Do not infer why a human person left the table.
- Do not force a PC's death, identity change or departure to tidy the roster.
- Do not choose voluntary actions for absent Player-owned characters.
- World continuity must not become an excuse to delay participation needlessly.
