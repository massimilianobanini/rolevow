The standard MIT wording mentioning "associated documentation" does **not** override the express CC BY 4.0 choice for standalone prose and prompts. A licence applies only to rights the licensor controls; no repository-wide licence can give away someone else's intellectual property.

This permission does **not** confer an endorsement by the original creator, the right to use trademarks as an official brand, or rights in any material outside the licensor's control.

## 4. Exclusions and boundaries

Excluded unless independently cleared: commercial adventure texts and stat blocks not covered by an applicable grant; non-SRD spells/classes/creatures; official art, maps, icons, fonts and music; trademarks; personal information; private testing transcripts; unpublished Google Drive documents; tester data; credentials; and any private business/financial information.

The expanded test scope prepares independent adapters for D&D 2014/SRD 5.1, D&D 2024/SRD 5.2.1 and the unofficial experimental Daggerheart™/SRD 2.0 adapter. The D&D SRD components require their own CC BY 4.0 source notices. **Daggerheart SRD 2.0 material and adaptations are subject to the applicable DPCGL 2.0 terms:** the RoleVow CC BY grant applies only to separable original work and cannot grant additional rights in third-party content. See [THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md](THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md).

**Brand rights are separate from copyright licences.** The RoleVow name, distinctive visuals and product identifiers are not automatically granted for official-looking derivative releases. Registration, exclusivity and historical "first" claims have not been verified.

## 5. Release gate (blocking)

Before publishing, verify and record: (1) original vs derivative vs third-party inventory for every file; (2) correct MIT/CC BY boundaries and contributor rights; (3) exact SRD attribution; (4) no non-SRD proprietary material or restricted media; (5) trademark/non-endorsement review; (6) cross-file consistency; and (7) the owner's explicit release approval after the full A–H pre-publication gate.

Original project materials are intentionally made available under the stated licences; third-party content remains governed by its own terms. This file is not legal advice.
