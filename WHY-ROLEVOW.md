# AI should not take away the part of RPGs you love

**The first Simple Tabletop RPG Tool, with the Negative Price Ultra-Guarantee.**

Adapted from [Divertoscopio's why-use guide](https://github.com/massimilianobanini/divertoscopio/blob/main/PERCHE-USARE-IL-DIVERTOSCOPIO.md). RoleVow is a free public AI-assisted toolkit for existing tabletop RPGs, not a new model or ruleset. No project credits or per-turn fees; ChatGPT limits still apply.

## Start with what you enjoy

Love drawing maps, writing NPCs, rolling physical dice or reading manuals? Keep doing it. Decide what to retain and what to delegate. There is no mandatory amount of AI.

| Situation | How RoleVow may help | What stays yours |
|---|---|---|
| You love inventing plots and NPCs | Check inconsistencies | Characters, twists, dialogue |
| You enjoy preparing encounters | Suggest environmental hazards or alternatives | Enemies, maps, tactics |
| Players are stuck in your existing adventure | Suggest clues and coherent consequences | How the world reacts |
| You have little time | Separate essential preparation from optional detail | The creative tasks you enjoy |
| No GM or group is available | Run an AI-assisted chat adventure | Your character choices and roleplay |
| Returning after a long break | Reconstruct game state from checkpoints | Human memory and decisions |
| You prefer physical dice | Interpret reported results | Rolling and choosing outcomes within the rules |

## Common objections

### Preparation is part of the fun.
Keep it. Ask for a second opinion only if useful.

### Why let a machine invent my story?
You do not have to. Bring your own plot and ask for feedback. Players keep voluntary character decisions. AI may still misunderstand or make mistakes.

### Isn't chat play just a text adventure?
It can feel similar. It aims to support natural-language choices, existing rules and ongoing consequences rather than only predefined commands. It is not necessarily superior to video games or human tables.

### Why not use ChatGPT, manuals or random tables directly?
You can. RoleVow packages guided setup, adaptation, rules and continuity procedures. General superiority over a good prompt or structured TXT file is not proven.

### I don't know prompt engineering.
You do not need to. Use the [landing page](https://massimilianobanini.github.io/rolevow/) or paste the GitHub link and “Let's begin.” in ChatGPT. Customize later if desired.

### I want real dice and my own choices.
Say “I'll roll my dice and tell you the results. Do not decide my character's voluntary actions.” The GM has final authority in human-led games.

### AI may slow down, forget facts or reveal spoilers.
These are real limitations. Verify consequential details and use checkpoints; never place GM-only secrets in chats or files accessible to players. See the [practical guide](COME-OTTENERE-IL-MASSIMO.md).

## When RoleVow might not help

If your human table already works without AI, if you enjoy the work others delegate, if you prefer audiovisual or face-to-face play, or if you require perfect accuracy, memory or full automation, another approach may fit you better. Use is voluntary.

## Try a real problem

GM: “I enjoy writing NPCs and plots. Don't write them for me. Help me identify where this investigation may stall and suggest alternatives.”

Player: “I'd like a solo fantasy tabletop RPG. I want to decide my character's actions and roll physical dice. Start with a few questions.”
