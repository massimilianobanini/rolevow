RoleVow is an English-language adaptation of the original Divertoscopio framework, designed as independently authored instructions and procedures for AI-assisted tabletop roleplaying. Its methods were informed by publicly available design discussions, books, play examples, practitioner experience and tests collected for the Italian project.

**A mention here is not an endorsement, affiliation, collaboration, sponsorship, permission to reuse someone else's work, evidence of direct training of a foundation model, or evidence of a gameplay result.** The actual copyright and third-party obligations for incorporated material are in [THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md](THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md) and [LICENSE-DOCS.md](LICENSE-DOCS.md).

## Creator and technical provenance

The Italian **Divertoscopio** was conceived and developed by Massimiliano Banini as a framework of original procedures, prompts and documentation. It is not a foundation AI model and did not train ChatGPT from scratch. RoleVow is the planned English project built from a specific, explicitly versioned Italian baseline. Changes to RoleVow must preserve attribution, distinguish local adaptation from the Italian source and undergo their own testing.

Research inputs for Divertoscopio included practical Game Master and player experience, campaign notes, public articles/videos and practitioner/community discussions, and comparisons of specific tabletop situations. No statement here establishes that the upstream project copied a source wholesale or used it as model training data. Actual-play notes support local hypotheses; they do not prove universal effectiveness or transfer English reliability scores.

## Books and game mastering references mentioned in the upstream credits

- *Dungeon Crawler Carl* — Matt Dinniman (tone, incentives, lasting consequences, comedic pressure).
- *Improv for Gamers* — Karen Twelves (collaboration and improvisation practices).
- *Mogworld* — Yahtzee Croshaw (diegetic knowledge and systemic consequence).
- *Orconomics* — J. Zachary Pike (institutional incentives, humor and emergent systems).
- *Play Unsafe* — Graham Walmsley (light prep, status, listening and improvisation).
- *The Lazy Dungeon Master* and *Return of the Lazy Dungeon Master* — Sly Flourish (efficient preparation and reusable material).
- *The Monsters Know What They’re Doing* — Keith Ammann (creature motivation and tactics).
- *Truth in Comedy* — Charna Halpern, Del Close and Kim Howard Johnson (improvisation, reintegration and shared memory).

## Play experience and evidence boundaries

The Italian project drew qualitative observations from its own table games, planned and executed tests, and other publicly available actual-play accounts. Player experiences may motivate a test but are not universal empirical proof. This page does not name private game participants or reveal transcripts. The English RoleVow candidate remains unvalidated in independent English play; do not turn Italian reports into English testimonials or transfer confidence percentages.
